# Glossary

- Outlet: an RSC restaurant/kitchen brand that owns a menu and fulfills
  sub-orders.
- Master order: the customer's unified order across one or more outlets.
- Sub-order: the outlet-specific fulfillment slice of a master order.
- Modifier group: a selection rule such as “Choose protein”.
- Modifier: an individual option such as “Extra chicken”.
- Settlement: a reviewed payout calculation for an outlet and accounting period.
- Central admin: trusted internal RSC staff using the Vite dashboard.
- Outlet POS: the separate Android application used by outlet operators.
